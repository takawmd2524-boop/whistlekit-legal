WhistleKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

WhistleKit does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

  • Matches, squads, player names, playing time and juggling results are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • The camera and microphone are used only on-device to count juggling touches and record clips you choose to save to Photos.
  • On Apple Watch, heart rate, distance and energy are read from and saved to Apple Health only with your permission, and never leave your devices.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Contact: see the support page.

日本語

WhistleKitは個人データを収集・送信・販売しません。データはすべて端末内（iPhone・Apple Watch・ヘルスケア）にのみ保存されます。
